## Intended use

This is forensic software, for examining evidence you are authorised to examine.
Reading a backup requires either that it is unencrypted or that you supply its
password; the library performs no password recovery and circumvents no access
control.

Whether you are authorised to examine a given backup is a question of law and of
your engagement, and this software neither establishes nor assumes it.
